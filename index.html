Bird Side Quests

Find something cool to see outside, from what birdwatchers near you reported this week.

What it explores

Recent bird sightings in New York City from two volunteer-run sources, eBird and iNaturalist: what was seen where, what is unusual for the place and season, and whether both sources back it up.

Who it's for

Beginner birdwatchers with a spare hour, who want a short outing nearby and a reason to pick one park over another.

How to use it

  1. Say where you are and how long you have, or pick your spot on the map.
  2. Watch the team search, then go and find the birds on your quest.
  3. Mark the ones you find. They are stamped in your field journal.
